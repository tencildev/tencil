Dictate comments and replies. With Whisper on your machine (whisper.cpp's `whisper-cli` and a model, or openai-whisper), the composer gets a mic: the browser records, and the daemon transcribes with your Whisper, so the audio stays on the machine. The box shows a level meter while you talk and "Transcribing…" while Whisper works. Settings → Voice switches it off and picks the model and language.
